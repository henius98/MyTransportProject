# Privacy Policy for MyTransportProject

**Effective Date:** October 16, 2026

**MyTransportProject** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our application.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you sign in or interact with MyTransportProject using your Google Account, we collect:

- **Basic Profile Information:** Your name, email address, and profile picture URL provided via Google OAuth.
- **Application Data:** Transport preferences, routes, or usage logs generated while using the application.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

- Authenticate your identity and manage your user session.
- Provide, operate, and maintain the core features of MyTransportProject.
- Respond to support inquiries and troubleshoot technical issues.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on encrypted databases and transmitted solely over secure HTTPS connections. We retain your personal information only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We **do not** sell, rent, or trade your Google user data or personal information to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. Data is only shared with infrastructure providers strictly necessary to host and run the application.

## 5. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

MyTransportProject's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

You may revoke MyTransportProject's access to your Google account at any time via your [Google Account Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). To request permanent deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.
